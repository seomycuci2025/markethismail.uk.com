How to Stop Chasing Casino Losses When Variance Hits

Understanding how to stop chasing casino losses is essential when facing inevitable cold streaks. Chasing losses, often referred to as tilt, occurs when a player increases bet sizes or deposits additional funds in an emotional attempt to recover lost money quickly.

This impulse is rooted in the Gambler’s Fallacy: the mistaken belief that past outcomes influence future independent random events. A slot machine that has not paid out for twenty spins has the exact same mathematical probability on the twenty-first spin.

Immediate Action Plan for Emotional Tilt

  • Enforce a mandatory 30-minute cool-off: Close your browser or mobile app and walk away physically.
  • Drink water or switch tasks: Reset your dopamine levels by focusing on unrelated daily routines.
  • Never deposit fresh cash on the same calendar day: Stick rigidly to your predetermined daily allowance.
